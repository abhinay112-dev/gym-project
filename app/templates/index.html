Workout Tracker
Log sets, reps and weights interactively. Mark each exercise done in real time with a visual progress ring.
Consistency Score
See your monthly consistency as a score out of 100. Streaks, missed days and rest days — all tracked.
Weekly Report
Bar chart view of your week — sessions completed, calories burned, total time, and how you compare to last week.

Log your energy, sleep quality, meal timing and session goals before every training session.
Post-Workout Review
Rate your session, log calories, note personal records and track recovery — every session, every time.
AI Assistant
Page-aware AI coach answers questions about any workout, form cues, muscle groups and technique — on every page.
